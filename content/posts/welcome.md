First entry.

A misplaced shot near the carrier earned a trip to a detention centre roughly
9,400 ly from home - which turned an ordinary evening into a long, slow
convergence: the carrier jumping toward me at ~490 ly a cycle, me jumping
toward it in a Federal Corvette at about a twentieth of that.

The arithmetic of a fleet carrier is unforgiving. Your ship contributes far
less than it feels like it does.
